Hermes DGX Spark

A private, self-hosted personal assistant for one household.

Hermes DGX Spark is a personal AI assistant that runs on a computer in our home. It is not a public product or service and is not offered to anyone outside the household.

When a household member signs in with Google, the assistant can help that person with their own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and contacts: reading and organizing mail, preparing drafts, and finding information. Each person signs in separately, and their data is kept in their own profile.

See the privacy policy for how Google user data is handled.
