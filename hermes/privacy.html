Privacy policy

Hermes DGX Spark · Last updated November 1, 2026

Hermes DGX Spark ("the app") is a personal assistant that runs on a computer owned by Dmitri Moore, in his home. Only members of his household use it, each with their own Google account. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

With your permission, the app can access the Google data covered by the permissions you approve on Google's consent screen. These may include Gmail messages and labels, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs and Sheets, and your contacts (read-only).

How the data is used

The app uses this data only to do what the signed-in person asks: for example, summarizing or organizing their email, preparing draft replies, checking their calendar, or finding a document. It is configured not to send email on anyone's behalf.

Where the data is stored

Google data and sign-in tokens are processed and stored on the household's own computer. Each person's data is kept in their own profile. Data is not stored on servers operated by others, except where Google itself stores it.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It is not read by anyone other than the signed-in person, except when needed for security, to comply with law, or with that person's consent.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your stored data deleted from the app, email the contact below.
