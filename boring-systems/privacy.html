Privacy policy

Boring Systems · Last updated November 12, 2026

Boring Systems ("the app") runs AI agents on a computer owned and operated by Dmitri Moore. Only one Google account signs in to the app: a dedicated account used by the agents, not a personal account. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

With the permissions approved on Google's consent screen, the app can access Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drive files available to the signed-in account. Only files explicitly shared with that account are available to it. The app does not request access to Gmail.

How the data is used

The app uses this data only to do the work the operator assigns to the agents, such as reading source documents and updating the business's knowledge base.

Where the data is stored and processed

Sign-in tokens are stored on the operator's own computer. To carry out a task, the agents send the relevant file content to the AI model providers they run on (Anthropic and OpenAI), which process it under their own terms. Data is not otherwise stored on servers operated by others, except where Google itself stores it.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by the app to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. It is shared with third parties only as described above, to provide the app's function, or when needed for security or to comply with law.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored data deleted, email the contact below.
