Boring Systems

Private, self-hosted AI agents for one small business.

Boring Systems runs AI agents on a computer owned and operated by Dmitri Moore. It is not a public product or service and is not offered to anyone else.

The agents sign in to Google with one dedicated Google account that only they use. With it they read and edit the Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drive files that have been explicitly shared with that account, to keep the business's own knowledge base up to date. No one else signs in to the app.

See the privacy policy for how Google user data is handled.
